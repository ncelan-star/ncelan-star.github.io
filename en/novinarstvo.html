Journalism

Nedjeljna Dalmacija · Heroina Nova · 1995–1997

His published journalism begins on 20 January 1995, in Nedjeljna Dalmacija, with an interview with Touch Friction. Everything in the list below comes after that date. The printed pieces are a record of the scene, not a TBF catalogue.

The full texts are in Croatian, as published, on nikolacelan.hr/novinarstvo.html. They are not translated here.

Nedjeljna Dalmacija, 1997 — TBF, Ping-pong

By Nikola Čelan. Photographs by Božo Vukičević. An interview with the recording lineup around the first album. Ana Brajdić’s yellow sidebar is not included in this record.

A conversation with the recording lineup of The Beat Fleet — Dragan Lukić Luky, Alex Dragi, Deluxe Dušo The Sumo Hrvat and Eazy L-Bee Medeni — around the album Ping Pong, umjetnost zdravog đira.

Nedjeljna Dalmacija, 20 January 1995 — Touch Friction

Pp. 32–33. Interviewed by Nikola Čelan. Participants: Toni Prug, Hrvoje Pelicarić. The lineup also included Mirjan Jovanović and Duško Mitić. Ilko Čulić’s sidebar on p. 33 is not included in this record.

An interview around the video Cut the Rope appearing on MTV, and around the band’s CD, Italian dates and a support slot for Carter USM in Zagreb.

Heroina Nova, p. 8 — No Comment: Još smo ljuti, ali nas je vrijeme iskvarilo

Interviewed by Nikola Čelan. In Rijeka: Teso (vocals, guitar), Torello (drums), Tommy (lead guitar); bassist Oleg was absent. Occasion: winning the Rijeka guitar competition (gitarijada).

A conversation with the Split band No Comment after that win.
